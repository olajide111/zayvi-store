# Storefront reading experience

The Blog & guides index uses published articles for the selected country. Search matches words in article titles and summaries. It does not search unpublished drafts or full article bodies. No results offers Clear search; Escape also clears the input. Published pages are excluded from the guide list.

The reading header offers Shop and Blog & guides. Individual pages have a Store breadcrumb and an Explore blog & guides link. The shared reading footer provides collection, contact, shipping, returns, FAQ, privacy and terms links. Every link retains the selected market, and the existing country-switcher rules remain in force.

An empty published list shows More to come. A backend failure shows a separate temporarily-unavailable message while help links remain accessible. The reading experience does not invent authors, ratings, dates or estimated reading times. Content bodies remain escaped plain text. Starter policy-page behaviour is unchanged.

Validation covers title/summary search, no matches, clear and Escape, exclusion of page entries, article navigation, market-preserving footer and collection links, empty-country and service-failure states, and phone/desktop horizontal overflow. Type checking, targeted lint and production build are required before Preview deployment.
