4 · ATELIER MINIMAL · SPEC

Assets, motion and how it fits the theme system

Required assets

Photography. One quiet, evenly lit image per piece on a plain warm background (4:5, 2400 px tall), plus one hero and two detail shots. A consistent crop matters more than volume.
Fonts. Cormorant Garamond 400, 500, 600 for headlines; Jost 400, 500, 600 for labels. Open licence, self-hosted.
Words. One line per collection and a short fabric note per product.
No decorative assets. The design is type, space and photography only.

Motion behaviour

Collection strip. Slides sideways 552 px (desktop) or 318 px (mobile) over 0.7 s, easing cubic-bezier(.22,.8,.2,1).
Bag. Slides in as a drawer (desktop) or sheet (mobile) in 0.4 s over a 35% dim, closes on Escape.
Everything else. Fades only. Nothing loops or autoplays.
Reduced motion. Strip and drawer switch instantly.

Navigation and page composition

Header. Three parts: two text links, the wordmark, and market plus bag. No mega menu.
Home. A full-height hero, one statement, three pieces.
Collection. One horizontal strip, one piece at a time, with arrow controls.
Product. A very large image and a narrow text column with Details and Delivery rows.
Bag. A drawer over the collection, so shoppers never leave their place.

Fit with the existing theme system

Template family. A new family, 'atelier', beside the existing six. Existing themes are untouched.
Tokens. Ivory, ink, stone, heading and body fonts, strip item width.
Best for. Small ranges (under about 30 pieces) of premium fashion, accessories or home goods.
Rules kept. No invented claims; WCAG 2.2 AA; 44 px targets; drawer traps focus and returns it on close.
Open points for the review. (1) Needs consistent, high-quality photography to work. (2) Not suited to large catalogues. (3) Nothing is built or applied; it needs sign-off from the named owner first.
