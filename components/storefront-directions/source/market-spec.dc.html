Assets, motion and how it fits the theme system

Required assets

Product photography. One clean square image per product (1200 px, light background) plus up to four more. Cards crop to 1:1, so keep the subject central.
Department tiles. One image or illustration per department (Fashion, Phones, Home, Beauty, Grocery and so on), 800 by 600 px.
Promo tiles. Three to five banners per week at 1200 by 480 px, with the text kept as live HTML (not baked into the image) so it can be translated and read by screen readers.
Seller marks. A square logo per seller, 160 px, with initials as a fallback.
Icons and fonts. Line icons in SVG; Plus Jakarta Sans 400, 600, 800 (open licence).

Motion behaviour

Departments menu. Opens as a panel that slides down in 0.2 s, closes on Escape.
Cards. Lift 4 px with a soft shadow on hover over 0.15 s. Quick add confirms with a short tick, not a pop-up.
Bag. Slides in from the right (desktop) or up from the bottom (mobile) in 0.25 s.
Tabs. The bottom tab underline slides in 0.2 s.
Principle. Fast and functional. Nothing loops and nothing autoplays; reduced motion turns all of it off.

Navigation and page composition

Header. Search first, then a Departments menu, market switch, account and bag. Mobile adds bottom tabs: Home, Departments, Search, Bag.
Home. Promo tiles, a department grid, rows of products and a seller strip.
Collection. A filter sidebar (department, price, seller, delivery to) and a dense product grid with sort.
Product. Gallery, price, seller block, quick facts, and more from the same seller.
Bag. Items grouped by seller, each with its own subtotal, then one order total.

Fit with the existing theme system

Template family. Extends general-commerce and wholesale with a marketplace section set: search header, department grid, promo tiles, product rail, seller strip, seller-grouped bag.
Tokens. Primary green, accent amber, surface and text colours, card radius, density (compact or comfortable).
Best for. Large catalogues, several sellers, everyday goods. Pairs with the vendor portal on the build plan.
Rules kept. Seller names are shown only where the seller is real and approved. No invented ratings, stock counts, or delivery promises; WCAG 2.2 AA.
Open points for the review. (1) Seller grouping needs the vendor portal (item 16 on the build plan) before it can be real. (2) Delivery-to and price filters need real catalogue and shipping data. (3) Nothing is built or applied; it needs sign-off from the named owner first.
