5 · FRESH MARKET · SPEC

Assets, motion and how it fits the theme system

Required assets

Product images. One square image per product on a light background (1200 px). Produce and staples can use a simple, consistent style.
Banner art. Two or three promo banners with live text over them.
Fonts. Nunito 400 to 900, open licence.
Product data (essential). Pack size, unit price per kg or litre, origin and storage notes for every item.

Motion behaviour

Steppers. Quantity changes update the basket total at once, with a 0.15 s highlight. No page reload.
Cards. Soft shadow on hover. No movement.
Mobile basket bar. Rises 40 px when the first item is added.
Reduced motion. The rise and highlight are removed.

Navigation and page composition

Aisles. A permanent left rail on desktop, scrolling chips on mobile.
Home. A banner with an add-the-whole-list button, then weekly staples with steppers on each card.
Aisle page. Dense list rows: image, name, pack size, unit price, stepper.
Product. Pack size, origin, storage and a substitution note.
Basket. Always visible on desktop; a delivery slot area on the review page.

Fit with the existing theme system

Template family. Extends the grocery family with the aisle rail, stepper rows, a persistent basket and a slot picker. Existing grocery theme untouched.
Tokens. Leaf green, tomato accent, cream surface, density.
Best for. Food, household goods and repeat orders with many small items.
Rules kept. Delivery slots are sample layout only; real slots come from seller delivery settings. No stock or freshness claims; WCAG 2.2 AA.
Open points for the review. (1) Unit pricing and pack sizes must exist in the catalogue. (2) Delivery slots and substitutions need the shipping engine (item 13 on the build plan). (3) Nothing is built or applied; it needs sign-off from the named owner first.
