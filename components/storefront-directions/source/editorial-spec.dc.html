Assets, motion and how it fits the theme system

Required assets

Photography (the key asset). Art-directed, portrait-led shots: one hero per issue (3:4, 3200 px tall), one lead image per product (4:5, 2400 px), plus two detail shots of the cloth and the construction. Natural light, one consistent colour grade per issue.
Textile swatches. A close crop of each cloth (square, 1200 px) for the Notes on the cloth panel. Credit the maker where known.
Textile band. A repeating pattern drawn in SVG, used as dividers and the page footer. Needs three to five patterns per brand, supplied or approved by the maker. Patterns that come from a specific community must be used with that community's or maker's agreement.
Fonts. Fraunces 400, 600, 800 for headlines and prices; Instrument Sans 400, 600, 700 for labels and body. Both open licence, self-hosted.
Words. An issue title, a short editor's note and a line on each cloth. Sample copy here is placeholder.

Motion behaviour

Image reveal. Hero and product images wipe in from the top over 1.2 to 1.4 s as they enter the screen, once only.
Slow drift. The hero image scales 6% to 12% and rises 18 px over 24 s. It is the only constant motion, and it stops entirely with reduced motion on.
Links and buttons. Underlines draw in over 0.2 s. No hover zoom on images.
Page change. Short cross-fade (0.3 s). No sliding.
Mobile. Same reveals, no drift, so scrolling stays smooth on low-cost phones.

Navigation and page composition

Masthead. Centred wordmark, an issue line, small-caps sections and a bag count. On mobile it collapses to Menu, market switch and Bag.
Home is a spread: a large image beside the issue title, a textile band, three numbered pieces at different sizes, a pull quote.
Collection is a three-column page with staggered image heights, numbered captions and text filters.
Product is image-led with the cloth story under the fold and related pieces as a row.
Bag reads like an order sheet: numbered lines, large totals, PayPal and Checkout.

Fit with the existing theme system

Template family. Builds on the existing editorial family. It adds a section set: masthead, issue hero, textile band, numbered feature, cloth notes, pull quote. Existing editorial themes are untouched.
Tokens. Paper, ink, accent, textile palette (3 colours) and the heading and body fonts.
Best for. Fashion, textiles, homeware and craft with strong photography and a story. Not suited to large catalogues.
Rules kept. No invented reviews, scarcity or delivery claims; WCAG 2.2 AA (contrast checked on indigo and cream, 44 px targets, visible focus, text equivalents for the band).
Open points for the review. (1) Images here are colour placeholders; the whole direction depends on real photography. (2) Textile patterns need maker approval before use. (3) Nothing is built or applied; it needs sign-off from the named owner first.
