Assets, motion and how it fits the theme system

Required assets

Cut-out garment images (the key asset). One image per product, the garment alone on a transparent background, front view, shot on a hanger or ghost mannequin. PNG or WebP with alpha, 1600 px tall, same crop and hanger position for every piece so the rail lines up. Add one extra image per colour.
Detail shots. Two close-ups per product (fabric, fastening) shown in the product panel.
Rail and floor. The rail bar, hanger and floor shadow are drawn in SVG and CSS, so no image is needed. A merchant can pick the floor colour and rail finish.
Fonts. Bricolage Grotesque 400, 600, 800 (open licence), self-hosted at launch.
Fallback. If a product has no cut-out, it shows a plain card on the rail rather than a broken image.

Motion behaviour

Rail glide. Arrows, swipe or keys move the focus. Garments slide 190 px (mobile) or 340 px (desktop) over 0.8 s, easing cubic-bezier(.22,.8,.2,1).
Pull-forward. Opening a product scales the garment up and moves it left while the others fade out. Same 0.8 s curve, so it reads as one movement.
Collection. Garments settle onto two or three rails in a grid; filtered-out pieces fade to 12%.
Panels. Info card, product sheet and bag rise 40 px with a fade over 0.5 s.
Reduced motion. With "reduce motion" on, all transitions drop to an instant 0.01 s cross-fade. Nothing autoplays.
Performance. Only transform and opacity animate; images lazy-load beyond the two nearest neighbours.

Navigation and page composition

Home is the stage itself: a focused garment, neighbours either side, a compact shopping panel. No menu bar.
Collection is the same stage with garments laid out on rails, filtered by room (category).
Product pulls one garment forward with colour, size and Add to bag.
Bag slides over the stage (a side panel on desktop, a bottom sheet on mobile) with quantity controls, subtotal, PayPal and Checkout.
Market. The £ / ₦ switch changes every price. Sample prices only.

Fit with the existing theme system

Template family. A new family, "showroom", beside editorial, general-commerce, product-specification, grocery, home-furniture and wholesale. The existing six are untouched.
Tokens. Floor colour, rail finish, accent, text colour and corner radius are tokens a merchant can edit.
Sections. Typed sections only: rail stage, room index, shopping panel, bag panel. No arbitrary HTML.
Best for. Clothing and accessories with clean cut-out photography. Not suited to large catalogues or items that need many specifications.
Rules kept. No invented reviews, stock or delivery claims; WCAG 2.2 AA (all controls are buttons, 44 px targets, visible focus, text labels on every garment).
Open points for the review. (1) The garments in these boards are drawn placeholders; the look depends on real cut-out photography. (2) A full-screen rail suits about 6 to 40 pieces per room; larger ranges should switch to the collection grid. (3) Nothing here is built or applied. It needs sign-off from the named owner first.
